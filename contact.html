Frequently Asked Questions

What are your admission requirements?

We require completion of entrance exams, submission of academic records, and a personal interview. Scholarships are available for academically outstanding and sports-talented students.

Do you offer boarding facilities?

Yes, we have separate boarding facilities for boys and girls with 24-hour supervision, nutritious meals, and recreational facilities.

What payment options are available?

We accept quarterly, semester, and annual payments. We also provide financial assistance and payment plans for eligible families.

Can I visit the campus?

Absolutely! We offer campus tours for prospective students and parents. Please schedule your visit by contacting admissions.
