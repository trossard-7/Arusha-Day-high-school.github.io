Our Academic & Extracurricular Programs

Comprehensive Learning Opportunities for Every Student

📚 Academic Excellence Program

Our rigorous academic curriculum is designed to develop critical thinking, creativity, and analytical skills. We follow international standards while maintaining local relevance, ensuring our students are well-prepared for global universities and careers.

Science & Mathematics

Advanced courses in Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and Mathematics with hands-on laboratory experience. Our STEM focus prepares students for engineering and medical careers.

Languages

English, Spanish, Mandarin, and French with native speakers. Students achieve language proficiency and cultural understanding.

Humanities

History, Geography, Economics, and Philosophy courses emphasizing critical analysis and global perspectives.

Business Studies

Comprehensive business education including economics, entrepreneurship, and international business practices.

🎨 Creative Arts Program

We believe in nurturing creativity and self-expression. Our comprehensive arts program includes visual arts, performing arts, music, and digital media, taught by industry professionals.

Visual Arts

Painting, drawing, sculpture, and digital art. Students develop artistic skills and create portfolios for art school admissions.

Performing Arts

Theater, dance, and drama productions. Students perform in annual concerts and plays in our modern 500-seat auditorium.

Music

Classical, contemporary, and world music. Our music program includes orchestra, band, choir, and individual lessons.

Digital Media

Video production, graphic design, animation, and photography using professional-grade equipment.

⚽ Sports & Wellness Program

Our comprehensive sports program develops athletic excellence, teamwork, and discipline. We offer 15+ sports and ensure every student participates in physical activity.

Team Sports

Football, basketball, volleyball, cricket, and field hockey with competitive teams and intramural leagues.

Individual Sports

Tennis, swimming, athletics, badminton, and martial arts for personal development and fitness.

Physical Education

Mandatory PE classes emphasizing fitness, wellness, and healthy lifestyle habits.

Mental Wellness

Yoga, mindfulness, and mental health awareness programs supporting student wellbeing.

💻 Technology & Innovation Hub

Prepare for the digital future with our cutting-edge technology programs. From coding to robotics, we equip students with 21st-century skills.

Programming

Python, Java, JavaScript, and web development. Students build real-world applications and contribute to open-source projects.

Robotics & AI

Hands-on robotics competitions and artificial intelligence exploration. Our robotics team competes nationally.

Data Science

Introduction to data analytics, visualization, and machine learning for aspiring tech professionals.

Cybersecurity

Digital safety and cybersecurity awareness to create responsible digital citizens.

🌍 Global Citizenship Program

We cultivate global perspectives and cross-cultural understanding. Students engage in international exchange programs and service projects.

International Exchange

Year-long and semester-long exchange programs with partner schools in 20+ countries.

Service Learning

Community service projects addressing local and global challenges. Students engage in meaningful volunteer work.

Model UN

Students represent countries in Model UN conferences and develop diplomacy skills.

Cultural Festivals

Annual celebration of diverse cultures through performances, food, and exhibitions.

🔬 STEM Innovation Program

Our advanced STEM program integrates Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics. Students engage in research and real-world problem-solving.

Research Projects

Students conduct independent research under faculty guidance, presenting findings at conferences.

Engineering Design

Design and build engineering solutions for real-world problems using CAD and prototyping.

Science Labs

Advanced laboratory work in biology, chemistry, and physics with modern equipment.

STEM Competitions

Participate in national and international STEM competitions and science olympiads.

🎯 Enrichment & Special Programs

Beyond the core curriculum, we offer numerous enrichment opportunities:

  • Leadership Development - Student council, prefect programs, and peer mentoring
  • Debate & Public Speaking - Competitive debate, public speaking clubs, and rhetoric training
  • Student Newspaper & Media - Student-run media covering school news and global issues
  • Clubs & Societies - 40+ clubs from photography to entrepreneurship
  • Mentoring Programs - Career mentoring and university guidance counseling
  • Advanced Placement - AP and IB programs for university-level coursework
